When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Horizon West

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.
