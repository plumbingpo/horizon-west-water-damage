How Drying Works on an Orlando Water Damage Job

Homeowners in Orlando often want to know what living with drying equipment is actually like. The first day covers extraction, taking out unsalvageable materials, and placing air movers and dehumidifiers where the water went. Expect noise on the level of a few big fans, plus noticeable warmth in the room. It has to run day and night, so please do not switch it off, move it, or open windows unless we ask you to.

Each day after that, a technician visits to take moisture readings in the same spots and record them. As areas dry, we move machines to the places that are lagging and take away the ones no longer needed. You will see the numbers and hear what they mean.

A typical job takes 3 to 5 days of drying. It can run longer when water sat for a while before it was found, when it reached insulation or hardwood, or when several floors are involved. Where Water Damage Starts in Orlando Properties

Some water problems need a phone call this minute, and others can safely wait until morning. We have ordered these five common Orlando calls by how fast each one needs attention.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Cold weather, depending on the season, is one trigger, with the leak appearing as the frozen section thaws. Close the main valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call right away, even at night. Ceilings that bulge with trapped water should be left alone and the room below kept empty.

Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. The harm continues, though, since water has already run under cabinets and flooring where air does not move. For Orlando kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. Puddles on the ground cover, dripping ductwork or wet insulation between the joists are what an inspection usually turns up. Left alone, the damp air rises into the rooms above and the wood framing begins to decay. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time. Avoid wiping or bleaching a big area on your own, as that sends spores into other rooms. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across Orlando.
